Privacy Policy

Effective from October 20, 2026 · Česky

In short

  • OtherEyes collects no personal data, requires no account and contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools. Nothing is sent to the author of the app or to third parties.

Camera

  • The camera is used only for the live preview with the vision effect. The image is processed on the phone (on the graphics chip) and is never uploaded or stored without your action.
  • The app does not use the microphone – recorded videos are silent.

Photos and videos

  • The app only adds photos and videos to your Photos library when you tap Save (add-only access). It never reads your library.
  • Photos and videos you do not save or share are deleted from the phone's temporary storage when you close the preview.

Vision profiles and vision check

  • Values you enter (age, diopters, astigmatism, names of family members), prescription history and the results of the approximate vision check are stored on this device (and, with iCloud sync turned on, in your iCloud).
  • They are not health records and are not shared. You can delete them in Settings → Restore default settings, or by uninstalling the app.

iCloud sync (OtherEyes Premium)

  • With Premium, vision profiles, the vision atlas and vision check results can be synced through your own iCloud account. They are then stored with Apple in your iCloud; the author of the app has no access to them. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings → iCloud sync. Display calibration is never synced.

Purchases

  • OtherEyes Premium is purchased through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the app only learns whether Premium is unlocked.

Sharing

  • Sharing happens only at your request through the phone's system share sheet; the app itself sends nothing.

Children

  • The app is suitable for school use and processes no data about children.
